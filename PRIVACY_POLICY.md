# Televo Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 14 September 2026_

This policy describes what Televo collects, why, and what control you have.
It is written to match what the app actually does — every item below
corresponds to real behaviour in the code.

## What we collect

**Account information.** Your email address (used to sign in), a username, and
a display name. Optionally a profile photo and a short bio, if you add them.

**Phone number.** Required when you create an account. It is used for one
purpose: letting people who already have your number in their address book
find you on Televo. Your number is never shown to other users and cannot be
read in bulk — matching happens by comparing cryptographic hashes, so another
user only ever confirms a number they already had. You can update or remove it
afterward in Settings — removing it only stops future contact matching; it
does not delete your account.

**Messages and media.** The messages, photos and files you send are stored so
they can be delivered and so you can see your history. In a **Secret Chat**,
message contents are encrypted on your device before they are sent and can
only be read by you and the person you're talking to — we cannot read them.

**Contacts (optional).** If you use "Find friends" and grant permission, the
app reads phone numbers from your address book **on your device**, converts
them to irreversible hashes, and sends only those hashes to check for matches.
Your contact list itself is never uploaded or stored.

**Usage and technical data.** When you were last active (if you leave "Last
Seen" enabled), and a push notification token for your device so we can
deliver notifications.

## What we do NOT do

- We do not sell your data.
- We do not use your data for advertising.
- We do not read the contents of Secret Chats — we cannot.
- We do not upload your address book.

## Calls

Voice and video calls connect directly between devices where possible, and
their media is encrypted in transit (DTLS-SRTP). Call **contents** are not
recorded or stored. We do store a call record (who, when, duration, whether it
was answered) so you can see your call history.

## Encryption

Regular chats are encrypted in transit and stored encrypted at rest by our
hosting provider. **Secret Chats** add end-to-end encryption: the encryption
keys never leave your device, and message contents are unreadable to us and to
our hosting provider. You can verify a Secret Chat has not been intercepted by
comparing safety numbers with the other person (chat menu → Verify
encryption).

## Your control over your data

- **Delete your account.** Settings → Delete account. This permanently erases
  your account, profile, messages, media and push tokens. It cannot be undone.
- **Privacy settings.** You can turn off Last Seen and read receipts in
  Settings → Privacy & Security.
- **Blocking and reporting.** You can block any user and report messages.
  Reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

## Data retention

Your data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account,
your data is deleted along with it.

## Children

Televo is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
data from them.
